I build apps for **iPhone and iPad**, and I teach other developers to build them too.

I've been shipping mobile apps since 2014, from mobile banking and digital wallets to on-demand services and games, and I've led iOS work at **DataArt**, **EPAM Systems** and **Multiverse**. In 2025 Apple certified me to teach its **App Development with Swift** curriculum, and I've lectured iOS at **BTU** since 2021. Today I'm at **Crocobet**, working across mobile, web and backend.

Outside the day job I make games and developer tools, contribute to open source, and publish my own projects in the open so others can learn from them.

## 🍎 iOS first

- **Swift and SwiftUI** by default, UIKit and Combine where they fit, Objective-C when the codebase calls for it
- **Architecture**: modular apps split into local Swift packages, TCA, XcodeGen, and separate Development / Staging / Production environments
- **The whole road to the App Store**: TestFlight, App Store Connect, subscriptions and in-app purchases, privacy labels, store listings and screenshots
- **Device features** such as nearby, phone-to-phone sharing over Bluetooth
